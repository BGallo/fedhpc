# Traveling salesman benchmarks

- `tsplib/dimacs-tsplib-large.zip` and `dimacs-large/` are the DIMACS
  collection of TSPLIB instances with at least 1,000 cities as of 2000.
  Source: <https://archive.dimacs.rutgers.edu/Challenges/TSP/download.html>.
  This is a subset, not the full TSPLIB.
- `national/bm33708.tsp` and `national/ch71009.tsp` are the Burma and
  China National TSP instances, downloaded 2026-11-26 from
  <https://math.uwaterloo.ca/tsp/world/countries.html>. Their status is
  maintained at <https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/tsp/world/summary.html>.
  These are long-term challenge instances, not initial solver tests.
